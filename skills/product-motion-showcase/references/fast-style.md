# Fast style

I work in two feels: subtle and fast. Subtle is my usual calm motion. Fast is quick and kinetic. Ask which one early (see `SKILL.md`). Everything else in the skill still holds.

## Subtle and fast

| | Subtle (my default) | Fast |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scenes | Long holds, one flow | About 1 to 1.5 seconds per phrase or clip. A hero scene can run up to about 3.5 seconds |
| Text | Masked line reveal (`text-animation.md`) | Kinetic text (below) |
| Cuts | Few, and overlapping | Hard cuts on the beat. The background flips between dark and light |
| Motion | 80/80 with small moves | 80/80 with shorter moves (start at 0.3 to 0.6 seconds), more motion blur, the subtle bounce where it fits |
| Assets | A card wall, quiet grids, a gallery | A floating image cloud, assembling into a mosaic |
| Music | Calm start, builds | A quick tempo, with cuts within about a tenth of a second of a beat |

## Kinetic text

- One short phrase per scene, two to five words. Tell the story in a few phrases: a statement, a turn and a reveal.
- The words come in one by one and fast, with a fade and a little blur or slide, about 0.1 to 0.2 seconds per word. The key word gets the accent colour.
- A phrase stays for about 1 to 1.5 seconds. Then a hard cut replaces it, and the background flips between dark and light from phrase to phrase.
- Stretched typing for a playful line: the letters are typed one after the other, the vowels are stretched so the word keeps growing, and the line slides sideways to keep its end in the middle. Then the line resolves into the logo.
- Keep the text readable. Put it in the middle and let the images sit around it, never under it.

## Image cloud

Assets drift in around a phrase with depth and a little blur, in different sizes and at different speeds, and then settle into a ring around the phrase. The whole move takes about 2.5 seconds.

## Assemble into a mosaic

The fast scene for a mosaic. See `presentation-scenes.md`: a row of icons pops in, scatters, and every icon becomes one tile that then fills with its content. About 2.5 seconds in total.

## Keyword scenes

For a promo of a service or a person. About 2 seconds per scene, on a bar:

- One keyword per scene.
- One hero visual made of simple shapes: a UI mock, a phone, a shape that morphs (a circle, a blob, a star, a flower), a chart that grows, a sphere of dots that turns into letters.
- A colour-block background that changes per scene, sometimes with a flash between scenes.
- A two-part headline: a heavy sans in capitals and below it an italic serif line in the accent colour.
- Small floating chips, a counter and corner marks as frame furniture.
- A glitch can swap one word for another.
- It ends on a name card with a tagline.

Ask for the four to six keywords, the accent colours, the name and the tagline.

## Cover

In the fast style the cover can be the logo with one illustration on the brand colour. A mosaic of panels also works (`presentation-scenes.md`). Never a block of text next to a grid of logos.

## What does not change

The 80/80 ease as the only curve, with the subtle bounce. Motion blur on everything that moves. Text first and the group after it, never at the same moment. Finished logos, no flicker. Whooshes at or below the music. The music is the clock: the cuts land on a beat.
