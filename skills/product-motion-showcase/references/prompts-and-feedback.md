## Prompts to start with

Replace the brackets with your own material.

**Interface video**

- "Make an interface video of my [app or website], around 15 seconds, in the [one-device] frame. Here are my screens, logo, three brand colours and font. Tell me the story back first and build nothing yet."
- "Animate my static screens subtly. Every move needs a reason, use the 80/80 ease, give a few elements a subtle bounce and turn motion blur on."
- "Open with a logo animation, then one flow through [screen A, B and C], and loop back to the start. Make frame 0 the best shot, taken from a still moment, and show me three candidates."

**Brand reel**

- "Make a brand reel for [brand], 16:9, around [60] seconds. Start with a statement, reveal the brand, explain it in parts, show it applied and end on the logo."
- "Use music at [120] BPM, so the length is whole bars. Start with a hook, no fade-in, and end with a stinger as the logo animates, no fade-out."
- "Keep the first scenes calm with long holds, and let the cuts speed up towards the end."

## Prompts that worked for me

These are phrased generally. Replace the brackets with your own material.

- "Here are my assets and storyboard. First check that you understand it and can see the sketches. Do not make anything yet."
- "Format like my existing portfolio frame, length in whole seconds."
- "Music in the mood of [reference video]: beeps at the start and a bass that goes 'dum dum dum'." Sending the file along helped, because it could be measured.
- "Do not animate step by step. Let step 2 start when step 1 is almost done, without overlap in view, smooth with easing."
- "Follow the lines longer, let the labels move with the camera, add some depth."
- "When zooming out, the distance may shrink, like an elastic."
- "Let the objects come in from the outside, half after each other, partly outside the frame."
- "Take over the rotating object from the screen recording and let it turn into the 3D objects."
- "Make an After Effects file so I can edit it myself."

## How I like to give feedback

For every point, give three things:

1. The time stamp.
2. What feels wrong: stutters, flickers, too fast, too close together.
3. What it should become.

Example: "0:12, the labels flicker when the camera moves. Keep them steady and let them slide along the line."

Spoken feedback with time stamps works fine.
